Application 2.2: Dimensions of Self-Concept

Grade 11 Life and Career Skills - Quarter 1, Lesson 2

Reminder of the 4 quadrants: Private-Actual ("How do I actually see myself?"), Private-Ideal ("How would I like to see myself?"), Social-Actual ("How do others actually see me?"), Social-Ideal ("How do I want others to see me?")

Scenario

Carlo is a Grade 11 student who plays guitar in a small band.

A: "Honestly, I'm not that confident performing live. I get nervous and I know my timing slips when I'm anxious."

B: "I want to get to a point where I can play a full set without checking the chord sheet, and where nerves don't throw me off."

C: "My bandmates probably think I'm the most reliable one in the group since I always show up early and know my parts."

D: "I want people at school to see me as someone who takes music seriously, not just a hobbyist who jams around."
