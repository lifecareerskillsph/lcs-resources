Application Activity 4: Emotional Intelligence Components

Grade 11 Life and Career Skills - Quarter 1, Lesson 2

Reminder of the 5 components: Self-Awareness, Self-Regulation, Motivation, Empathy, Social Skills (Goleman, 1996)

Scenario

A group of four students is finishing a project the night before it's due. One member, Reign, has not submitted her part.

  1. Andrei notices his chest getting tight and realizes, "I'm getting frustrated, and it's mostly because I'm anxious about our own grade, not really about Reign."
  2. Instead of messaging Reign angrily right away, Andrei waits ten minutes and rereads his message before sending it.
  3. Andrei reminds himself that the group has hit deadlines before and keeps working on his own part instead of spiraling.
  4. When Reign finally replies that her grandmother was hospitalized that afternoon, Andrei immediately drops his irritation and asks if she needs help instead.
  5. Andrei proposes a plan to the group: he will cover Reign's section for tonight, and they will redistribute credit fairly in the reflection log.
