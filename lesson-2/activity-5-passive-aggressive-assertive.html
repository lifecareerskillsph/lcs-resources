Application Activity 5: Passive, Aggressive, or Assertive?

Grade 11 Life and Career Skills - Quarter 1, Lesson 2

Reminder: Passive avoids expressing needs out of fear of conflict. Aggressive expresses needs while violating or disrespecting others' rights. Assertive expresses needs, boundaries, and feelings clearly while still respecting others.

Scenario

Three students respond differently after a groupmate submits his part of a project two days late, causing the whole group to lose points.

Student 1: "It's fine, don't worry about it." (Says nothing further, but avoids the groupmate for the rest of the week and complains to other classmates instead.)

Student 2: "You always mess things up for everyone. Why should we even let you stay in this group?"

Student 3: "I was frustrated when your part came in late because it cost us points. Can we agree on a check-in the day before it's due next time?"
