Application Activity 1: Self-Concept vs. Identity

Grade 11 Life and Career Skills - Quarter 1, Lesson 2

Scenario

Jasmine, 17, is filling out a getting-to-know-you form for her new volleyball team. Here is what she wrote:

  1. "I'm 5'6", I have curly hair, and I'm pretty good at spiking."
  2. "I want to become a nurse someday because I think healing people matters more than almost anything else."
  3. "I'm usually the quiet one in a new group until I get comfortable."
  4. "Being part of my barangay's youth council is a big part of who I am. I take that role seriously."
  5. "I like painting and I'm interested in true crime podcasts."
  6. "I believe that people deserve a second chance, and that shapes how I treat others."

Part A: Sort Jasmine's Statements

1. "I'm 5'6", I have curly hair, and I'm pretty good at spiking."

2. "I want to become a nurse someday because I think healing people matters more than almost anything else."

3. "I'm usually the quiet one in a new group until I get comfortable."

4. "Being part of my barangay's youth council is a big part of who I am. I take that role seriously."

5. "I like painting and I'm interested in true crime podcasts."

6. "I believe that people deserve a second chance, and that shapes how I treat others."
