# Life & Career Skills Resources

Instructional resources for Grade 11 Life and Career Skills, including teacher guides, student materials, lesson presentations, assessment resources, and supporting tools aligned with the DepEd Strengthened Senior High School Curriculum for Life and Career Skills (2025).

## What Changed in This Version

The August 2026 version includes:

- progress-based grading in place of the earlier binary grading approach
- updated student-name handling for different name formats
- dropdown fields for Grade and Section
- chip dropdowns for selected tracker fields
- removal of redundant response options
- review and revision of reflection questions for better alignment with the Quarter 1 learning competencies

## Quarter 1 Understanding and Strengthening the Self

### Lesson 1: Key Developmental Stages
 
Current version: **September 2026**

[**Lesson 1 Slides**](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/10eeJ73tHlLeb5eyKpMjiLwrUx0XuQvkAK-LeSwHr-js/edit?usp=sharing)
Introductory presentation for Week 3, covering the four developmental lenses ahead of the interactive study guide and the two activities below.

[**Lesson 1 Interactive Study Guide**](https://lifecareerskillsph.github.io/lcs-resources/lesson-1/lesson1.html)
Self-paced scrollable guide covering physical, cognitive, psychosocial, and moral development, including concrete versus formal operational reasoning, seriation, classification, equilibration, and the distinction between Piaget's preoperational egocentrism and adolescent egocentrism. Built-in practice tests throughout with self-check answer keys.

Two interactive worksheets extend the moral development portion of the lesson: a facilitated-mode toggle supports teacher-led delivery, and a self-paced mode supports asynchronous ICLEX use. Formative, not scored, unless later adapted with an explicit rubric.

### Resources

[**The Heinz Dilemma**](https://lifecareerskillsph.github.io/lcs-resources/q1-moral-development/heinz-dilemma.html)
Single-select instrument mapped to Kohlberg's six stages and three levels of moral reasoning, with immediate feedback on selection.

[**The Porcupine and the Moles**](https://lifecareerskillsph.github.io/lcs-resources/q1-moral-development/porcupine-moles.html)
Fixed-choice resolution activity built for classroom polling, six possible resolutions mapped to justice, care, or integrative reasoning orientations, connected to strand-relevant career fields.
