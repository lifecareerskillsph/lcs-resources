# Life & Career Skills Resources

Instructional resources for Grade 11 Life and Career Skills, including teacher guides, student materials, lesson presentations, assessment resources, and supporting tools aligned with the DepEd Strengthened Senior High School Curriculum for Life and Career Skills (2025).

## Lesson Study Guides

Self-paced study guides for Grade 11 students, aligned to the Quarter 1 Learning Competencies.

**[Lesson 1](lesson-1/lesson1.html)** — Key Developmental Stages: Understanding and Strengthening the Self
**[Lesson 2a](lesson-2/lesson-2a.html)** — Developmental Tasks and Physiological Development
**[Lesson 2b](lesson-2/lesson-2b.html)** — Psychosocial and Emotional Development
**[Lesson 3a](lesson-3/lesson-3a.html)** — Fitness and Mindfulness
**[Lesson 3b](lesson-3/lesson-3b.html)** — Recreational Activities
**[Lesson 4](lesson-4/lesson4.html)** — Applying Life Skills to Different Domains

Each guide includes a Facilitated mode (for onsite, teacher-led delivery) and a Self-Paced mode (for independent student work), toggled at the top of the page.

## What Changed in This Version

The August 2026 version includes:

- progress-based grading in place of the earlier binary grading approach
- updated student-name handling for different name formats
- dropdown fields for Grade and Section
- chip dropdowns for selected tracker fields
- removal of redundant response options
- review and revision of reflection questions for better alignment with the Quarter 1 learning competencies
